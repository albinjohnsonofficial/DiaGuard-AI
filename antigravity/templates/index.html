Course Outcome (CO5) Alignment & Clinical Rationale

Course Outcome Statement:

"CO5 – Solve real life problems using appropriate machine learning models and evaluate the performance measures"

1. Real-Life Problem Formulation

Diabetes Mellitus affects over 537 million adults globally. Late detection causes severe renal, ophthalmologic, and cardiovascular degradation. Building an automated screening tool directly addresses a real-world clinical challenge.

2. Appropriate Model Selection (SVM)

Support Vector Machine with Radial Basis Function (RBF) kernel is ideally suited for moderate-sample medical data (n=768) where biological decision boundaries are inherently non-linear and complex.

3. Data Leakage Prevention

Physiological zero imputation and feature scaling (StandardScaler) are encapsulated inside a scikit-learn Pipeline, ensuring zero data leakage between training and validation folds.

4. Rigorous Performance Evaluation

Going beyond simple test accuracy, models are evaluated with 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation, Precision, Recall (Sensitivity), F1-Score, Confusion Matrices, and ROC-AUC curves.

Clinical Cost of Errors: Why Recall Matters Most

In medical diagnostics, False Negatives (Type II error) are exponentially more damaging than False Positives (Type I error). A False Positive simply prompts follow-up confirmatory laboratory tests (HbA1c/FPG) with minor inconvenience. A False Negative mistakenly reassures an undiagnosed diabetic patient, allowing irreversible microvascular damage to progress unchecked. Therefore, maximizing Recall (Sensitivity) while maintaining strong Precision was the primary selection criterion for our final SVM pipeline.
